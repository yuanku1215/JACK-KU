Technical Workflow (Implementation)

1. Geodetic Control Framework

Establishment of a high-precision spatial reference network:

  • GNSS Positioning: Static relative positioning in TWD97 [2020] linked to national tracking stations.
  • Geodetic Leveling: Precision leveling under the TWVD 2001 system connected to primary bench marks.
  • Traverse Surveying: Network densification in high-density areas to ensure sub-centimeter accuracy.

2. UAV Photogrammetric Pipeline

Aerial data acquisition and 3D surface reconstruction:

  • Aerial Photography: High-resolution imagery acquisition with sub-10cm GSD and ground control signal placement.
  • Aerotriangulation (AT): Rigorous bundle block adjustment to produce True Orthophotos.
  • Model Generation: Automated extraction of DSM/DEM and CityGML LOD-1 building geometries.

3. Numerical Topographic Mapping

Production of 1/1000 scale digital topographic assets:

  • Terrestrial Surveying: Field data collection using Total Stations for areas occluded by aerial sensors.
  • Digital Compilation: Compilation of standard DWG/DXF layers including contours and feature annotations.
  • Quality Control: Rigorous attribute and geometric verification following municipal mapping standards.

4. Web GIS & SDG Analytics

System development and sustainability thematic mapping:

  • Database Integration: Organization of feature classes (SHP) and comprehensive TWSMP metadata.
  • SDG Implementation: Specialized analysis of rooftop solar PV area and other sustainability metrics.
  • Web Platform: Interactive 3D campus navigation system providing visualized spatial queries.

Core Deliverables

This project resulted in a professional-grade spatial information suite, ensuring high interoperability for campus facility management and sustainable urban planning.
