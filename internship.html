Overview

This capstone internship is a two-month, full-time summer program bridging my junior spring and senior year. Unlike a single-assignment course, the capstone mirrors real-world geomatics practice: we start from field data acquisition, ensure quality control, then build a complete mapping product that is usable for analysis and communication.

What makes it capstone-level: it is end-to-end (field → processing → product), requires accuracy awareness, and forces consistent coordinate control so that 3D models and GIS layers align in the same spatial reference frame.

Workflow: Field → Processing → Delivery

1) Planning & Control

Define coordinate reference, prepare control points, and ensure alignment across all instruments.

2) Field Survey

Leveling for elevation, Total Station for geometry, and GNSS for robust georeferencing.

What I Learned

  • End-to-end thinking: field data quality directly controls the credibility of final visual outputs.

  • Measurement mindset: good maps come from validated measurements, not only nice rendering.
