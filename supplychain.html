Methodological Representative

A typical model structure utilized to ensure speed and debugging efficiency in large-scale discrete problems.

SETS:
  I /S1..S3/: supply;
  J /C1..C5/: demand;
  LINK(I,J): cost, x;
ENDSETS

MIN = @SUM(LINK: cost * x);
@FOR(I: @SUM(J: x(I,J)) <= supply(I));
@FOR(J: @SUM(I: x(I,J)) >= demand(J));
